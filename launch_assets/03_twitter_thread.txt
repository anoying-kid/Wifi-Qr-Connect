TWEET 1:
I was so tired of manually typing long Wi-Fi passwords on my Mac while my Android phone had a QR code right there. The friction between macOS and Android is real.

So I spent the last few weeks building a native macOS app to fix this. It's free & open source 🧵👇

[Attach Video or image 01_qr_scanner.png here]

TWEET 2:
I originally built a prototype in Flutter, but it was a heavy 43MB beast for a tiny utility. 😅

Today I'm releasing the complete rewrite: 100% native SwiftUI. 
It's lightning-fast, respects battery life, and the app size plummeted down to ~3MB. 

TWEET 3:
Features:
📷 Point your Mac's camera at your phone to scan and connect instantly
🔗 Generate QR codes for the network your Mac is connected to
📜 Saves your network history natively
