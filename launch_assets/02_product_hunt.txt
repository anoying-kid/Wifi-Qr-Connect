PRODUCT NAME:
WiFi QR Connect

TAGLINE (60 chars max):
Scan Wi-Fi QR codes on Mac. Bridge the Mac/Android gap.

DESCRIPTION (260 chars max):
The native macOS app that bridges the gap between your Mac and Android phone. Scan any Wi-Fi QR code with your camera to connect instantly, or generate a QR code to share your own network effortlessly. 100% native SwiftUI, lightweight (~3MB), and open source.

PRICING:
Free

CATEGORY:
Utilities, Developer Tools, Mac

FIRST COMMENT (Maker's Comment):
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm the maker of WiFi QR Connect. A Mac was my very first laptop, but as an Android user, I constantly hit a frustrating friction point: trying to connect my Mac to a friend's Wi-Fi network meant staring at identical SSIDs and typing ridiculously long passwords. My Android phone could scan a QR code to connect instantly—why couldn't my Mac?

I originally built a prototype in Flutter, but it was a heavy 43MB beast. Today, I'm launching the complete rewrite: 100% native SwiftUI. It's lightning-fast, respects your battery life, and plummeted down to just ~3MB.

Features:
📷 Scan Wi-Fi QR codes using your Mac's camera
🔗 Generate QR codes for your current network
📜 Secure history of past networks

It's completely free and open source. I built this to solve my own daily headache, and I hope it helps you too. I'd love to hear your feedback!
